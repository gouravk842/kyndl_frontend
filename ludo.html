📘 How to Play

Quick Rules

  • Roll the dice on your turn. A 6 can bring a token out of base and gives an extra roll.
  • Tap any glowing token to move it. Tokens animate one cell at a time.
  • Land on an opponent on a non-safe cell to capture and send them back to base.
  • ⭐ Star cells are safe. Tokens cannot be captured on safe cells.
  • Get all 4 tokens to the center home triangle to finish.
  • In Activity Mode, special triggers open a fun challenge popup.
